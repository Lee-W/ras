# Wei style study: sources and coverage

Review date: 2026-10-05. This record supports `profiles/wei.md`. It contains original observations, source metadata, and PDF hashes; it does not contain slide text extracts or third-party assets.

## Scope and method

- Collection: [Wei's SpeakerDeck profile](https://speakerdeck.com/leew), including all three listing pages. Listing positions below preserve the reviewed newest-first order rather than a moving definition of “the first three”.
- Included: positions 4–37, 34 PDFs, 3,175 pages. Page totals were checked against the downloaded PDFs.
- Excluded: positions 1–3, as requested. Those decks were not used as style evidence.
- Every included page was rendered into a contact sheet and visually inspected for layout, sequence, and progression. Extracted text was read alongside it; identical repeated text was consolidated in some earlier talk families without removing their visual reveal states.
- Nine selected recent groups (36 pages) were additionally enlarged: position 4 pages 34, 35, 36, 37, 40, 41, 43, 46; position 5 pages 96, 103, 104, 113, 115, 116, 117, 123; position 7 pages 9, 10, 13, 14; position 9 pages 175, 177, 179, 180; position 12 pages 19, 21, 25, 30, 64, 65, 66, 70, 73, 75, 86, 87.
- Coverage is a style reading of all pages with selected close views, not a full-resolution transcription/OCR audit of every image or validation of all code, claims, image permissions, or live delivery.
- Year weights are editorial synthesis priorities: 2025 = 1.00, 2024 = 0.75, 2023 = 0.50, 2020 = 0.25, 2019 = 0.20, 2018 = 0.15, 2017 = 0.10, 2016 = 0.05. They were not used to compute a statistical frequency score.
- Dates below are SpeakerDeck publication/upload metadata, not independently verified event dates. Related editions were read individually and interpreted as one evidence family; page count was not used as a vote.
- Downloaded PDFs, extracted text, and rendered images were kept outside this repository. Each SHA-256 identifies the exact PDF reviewed; a later upload at the same URL may differ.
