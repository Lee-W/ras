# Licence scope / 授權適用範圍

## Your presentation

You choose the licence for your own presentation text and original assets.
Images and other material supplied by third parties keep their own terms;
record their sources, use basis, and required credits in `sources.md`.

The project's `package.json` starts with `UNLICENSED`: you have not selected a
distribution licence for your content. This does not change the licences of
the included tools or dependencies.

## Included RAS tools and templates

RAS uses the standard **MIT licence**, preserved in [ras/MIT.txt](ras/MIT.txt).
It covers the copied RAS build scripts, original starter content, theme, and
guides. [ras/NOTICE.md](ras/NOTICE.md) explains the project's unofficial status
and third-party rights. These files do not assign a licence to your own slides
or grant permission to use third-party images.

Keep this directory when distributing the included RAS files. Build/export
copies it to `dist/licenses/`. Font notices are preserved separately in
`dist/assets/fonts/`. The same scope applies in both the project and its output.

## 臺灣華語

**你的簡報由你決定授權。** 你撰寫的文字與原創素材可自行選擇授權；
第三方圖片與其他素材保留各自的條款，使用依據與必要標示記錄在 `sources.md`。

`package.json` 起始設定為 `UNLICENSED`，表示你的內容尚未指定散布授權，
不會改變隨附工具或相依套件本身的授權。

隨附的 **RAS 工具與範本採標準 MIT 授權**，全文在 [ras/MIT.txt](ras/MIT.txt)。
適用範圍是複製過來的建置腳本、原創起始內容、主題與指南；
[ras/NOTICE.md](ras/NOTICE.md) 說明非官方身分與第三方權利。
這些檔案不會替你的投影片指定授權，也不授予第三方圖片的使用權。

散布隨附的 RAS 檔案時，保留本目錄。建置與匯出會複製到 `dist/licenses/`；
字型聲明另保留在 `dist/assets/fonts/`。專案與產出中的授權適用範圍相同。
